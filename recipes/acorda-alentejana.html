Açorda à Alentejana (Portuguese Garlic and Cilantro Soup)

Description

Açorda à Alentejana, or simply, Açorda is a staple of Alentejan cuisine. It's an old recipe from the arab occupation of Portugal and present in texts from the 8th to the 13th century.

Açorda is a bread soup and was considered a struggle meal. However, both low and highborn people seem to have enjoyed it. This is recipe is for the Alentejan variation of it.

Ingredients

  • 100 g cilantro
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • 1.5 L of boiling water
  • 4 eggs
  • 400 g sliced, Alentejan bread

Steps

  1. Crush the cilantro, garlic cloves and salt in a mortar until forming a paste.
  2. Place the paste in a pot.
  3. Drizzle olive oil over the mixture and pour the boiling water after.
  4. Poach the eggs in another pot.
  5. Place the bread slices in plates, pour the soup over the bread.
  6. Place the poached eggs on top.
  7. Serve immediately.
