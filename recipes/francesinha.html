Francesinha

Description

The francesinha might just be one of Portugal's best-known dishes. It is synonymous with the city of Porto and for good reason. It's just good for the soul (not so much the body).

Its history dates back to the 20th century. It is said to be an adaptation of a popular French sandwich for Portuguese tastes, hence its name francesinha or "little French lady".

Ingredients

For the sauce:

  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, chopped
  • 20 ml olive oil
  • 200 ml tomato sauce
  • 200 ml beer
  • 100 ml beef stock
  • 15 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and pepper

For the sandwich:

  • 2 thick slices of loaf bread
  • 1 linguiça
  • 1 fresh sausage
  • 2 thin slices of ham
  • 2 slices of mortadella
  • 1 thin steak
  • 5 slices of Flamengo cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 egg
  • French fries, to serve

Steps

For the sauce:

  1. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and garlic cloves. Cook for 5 minutes, until soft.
  2. Stir in the tomato sauce. Let it cook for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Pour in the beer and beef stock. Stir.
  4. Add the Worcestershire sauce, mustard, bay leaf and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Lower the heat and let it simmer uncovered for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Remove the bay leaf.
  7. Blend until smooth.
  8. Taste the sauce and adjust the seasoning if necessary. Keep it warm until serving.

For the sandwich:

  1. Toast the bread lightly, keeping its thickness.
  2. Cook the steak in a pan and set it aside.
  3. Cut the linguiça and the sausage lengthwise and cook them in the same pan.
  4. Place a slice of bread on an oven-safe deep plate.
  5. Lay the slices of ham and mortadella over the slice of bread.
  6. Place the sausage and linguiça over the previous layer.
  7. Place the steak over the sausage and linguiça.
  8. Place the second slice of bread over the structure and cover it with cheese slices.
  9. Place the francesinha under the broiler until the cheese melts.
  10. Fry the egg in the same pan as the steak.
  11. Remove the francesinha from the oven, top it with the fried egg and cover it in sauce.
  12. Serve with fries on the side.
